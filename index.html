Values & Preferences

How I work — and what I'm looking for.

What drives my decisions, how I show up in a team, and the kind of work I want to do next.

People first, always

Products exist to solve people's problems. I start every project by understanding who I'm building for and what's actually making their life harder. The best roadmap is useless if it doesn't connect to a real human need.

Take care of the team

A PM's job isn't just shipping features — it's creating clarity so engineers, designers, and stakeholders can do their best work. I protect the team from noise, fight for realistic scope, and make sure everyone knows why we're building what we're building.

Only build what matters

I'd rather ship one thing that solves a real problem than ten features nobody asked for. Saying no is a product skill. If I don't believe in what we're building, I'll say so — and propose what we should build instead.

Products that leverage technology

I'm drawn to products where we can use current technology — AI, automation, smart integrations — to create real leverage. Whether it's an enterprise platform or a fast-moving startup, I want to work on something interesting where the tools match the ambition.

Autonomy with accountability

I want ownership of product decisions, not just backlog grooming. In return, I'll own the outcomes — good and bad — and communicate openly.

Products I believe in

I do my best work on products that solve real problems. Enterprise AI, data platforms, tools that make people's work easier — that's where I thrive. I've turned down work on products I didn't believe in, and it's always been the right call.
